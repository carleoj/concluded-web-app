# Concluded

**A deterministic job analyzer for developers.**

Concluded extracts technical requirements from job descriptions and compares them against a developer's tech stack to help determine how well a job matches their technical skills.

Concluded uses rule-based analysis rather than generative AI, providing predictable and reproducible results.

## What It Does

Job descriptions can be unnecessarily long, repetitive, and filled with information that is not relevant to a developer's technical decision.

Concluded focuses on what matters:

- What technologies does the company use?
- What technologies are required?
- What technologies are preferred?
- How well does the job match my stack?

Instead of spending time reading and manually comparing requirements, Concluded gives you a direct technical assessment.

## Features

- **Tech Stack Matching** — Compare job requirements against your selected technologies.
- **Deterministic Analysis** — Uses predictable, rule-based logic instead of generative AI.
- **Technology Extraction** — Identifies relevant programming languages, frameworks, databases, tools, and technologies from job descriptions.
- **Fast Results** — Quickly determine whether a position is technically relevant.
- **Simple Interface** — Focused on one task without unnecessary complexity.
- **Developer-Focused** — Built specifically around the technical information developers care about.
